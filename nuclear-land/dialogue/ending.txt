ending good
The squad brings Abel Voss up out of the dark.

In the town square, Hesper reads out the tank numbers. Jory tells what he saw at the valve. The
Keepers break open the storehouse and find the drums, stamped with the town hall's mark.

Corwin smiles to the very end. It doesn't help.

The coolant runs where it should. The reactor steadies, and Cinderhill keeps its lights. For now.

ending open
Voss climbs into the daylight, blinking.

The mayor shakes his hand and calls it a miracle. Nobody asks where the water went. By morning
the tap on the coolant line is gone.

Voss knows. You know. The reactor sputters on, and Cinderhill's fate is still open.
